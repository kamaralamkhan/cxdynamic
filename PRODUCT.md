# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Enterprise customer-experience leaders, contact-center directors, operations leaders, and technology decision-makers evaluating how to modernize service, sales, and collections across voice and digital channels.

## Product Purpose

CXdynamIQ is an AI-powered omnichannel customer-experience and contact-center platform. It brings AI agents, human-agent copilots, routing, quality assurance, conversation intelligence, and real-time analytics into one operating layer so organizations can resolve customer needs consistently across channels. CXQ360.ai is the product's demo, trial, and customer evaluation experience.

## Positioning

CXdynamIQ presents AI-led, AI-assisted, and human-led service as one connected operating continuum. Its differentiating mechanism is contextual orchestration: customer intent and conversation context move across channels and between AI and people, with quality and analytics learning from every outcome.

## Operating Context

Buyers evaluate the platform through the public website, enterprise demos, and the CXQ360.ai trial journey. The platform sits alongside CRM, helpdesk, ticketing, ERP, messaging, communications, identity, and custom enterprise systems. Primary workflows include customer support, collections, sales and lead qualification, inbound and outbound engagement, agent assistance, quality management, and operational analytics.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- AI voice agents, AI chat agents, agent assist and copilot workflows.
- Intelligent routing, IVR, inbound and outbound calling, and preview, progressive, and predictive dialers.
- Omnichannel engagement across Voice, WhatsApp, SMS, Web Chat, Messenger, Viber, and Email.
- Conversation intelligence, AI quality assurance, real-time analytics, and workforce optimization.
- REST APIs, webhooks, SSO, SIP, and custom enterprise application integration.
- Industry and workflow relevance across banking, insurance, telecom, e-commerce, retail, logistics, healthcare, travel and hospitality, education, collections, sales, and customer support.
- The public site must remain a statically exportable Next.js application and preserve its existing routes, metadata, accessible interactions, and responsive behavior.

## Brand Commitments

- Product names: CXdynamIQ and CXQ360.ai.
- Preserve the supplied CXdynamIQ logo and its signal-blue, teal, lime, and deep-navy color cues.
- The website should feel like a modern, high-trust enterprise SaaS sales experience in the cloud communications and contact-center category while remaining original to CXdynamIQ.
- Navigation must make the full services, integrations, solutions, industries, and use cases easy to find; it must not collapse the product into a limited menu.
- Replace generic static-chatbot positioning with an interactive explanation of agentic AI that can understand, reason, act, resolve, and learn.

## Product Principles

1. Demonstrate orchestration rather than describing generic AI.
2. Keep enterprise capability broad but navigation and page narratives legible.
3. Show human and AI collaboration as one continuous operating model.
4. Make integrations and channels explicit, recognizable, and easy to scan.
5. Use conservative claims and label illustrative product demonstrations honestly.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Interactive controls must be keyboard accessible, visibly focused, operable without hover, compatible with reduced-motion preferences, and usable on touch targets of at least 44 by 44 pixels. Text and controls must meet WCAG AA contrast expectations.
