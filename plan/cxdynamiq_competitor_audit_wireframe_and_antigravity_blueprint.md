  * Exotel presents its **House of AI** ([House of AI](https://exotel.com/products/ai-and-automation/)) as a 5-tab modular stack: **Intelligent Voice Agents** ([Gen-AI Voice Agents](https://exotel.com/products/gen-ai-voice-agents/)), **Intelligent Chat Agents** ([Gen-AI Chat Agents](https://exotel.com/products/gen-ai-chat-agents/)), **Conversation Intelligence (CQA)**, **Exotel MCP Server** ([Exotel MCP Server](https://exotel.com/products/exotel-model-context-protocol/)), and **Bot Designer**.

  * Underlying voice AI is powered by [AgentStream](https://exotel.com/products/agentstream-voice-streaming/), which highlights a `<20 ms` media path latency so LLMs keep the bulk of the `600–800 ms` conversational turn-taking budget.
* **Multi-Channel Context Preservation (WhatsApp/Chat to Voice):**
  * Exotel dedicates a full product page to its **Conversational Context Data Platform (CCDP)** ([Exotel CCDP](https://exotel.com/products/ccdp-unified-customer-profiles/)) and explains in its [Harmony CCDP Architecture Post](https://exotel.com/blog/exotel-harmony-platform-conversational-context-data-platform-ccdp/) that CCDP tracks three live signals across every touchpoint: **State** (live conversation summary and timeline), **Vibe** (continuous emotional state from voice pitch/tone and text), and **Intent** (what the customer is trying to accomplish right now).

A CCDP tells you what they need right now"* ([Exotel CCDP](https://exotel.com/products/ccdp-unified-customer-profiles/)). When a customer switches from WhatsApp or a bot to a voice call, *"Instead of asking 'How can I help you?', the agent should begin with contextual understanding: 'I see you were trying to block your card. Let me help you.'"* ([Sachin Bhatia Harmony Feature](https://exotel.com/newsroom/sachin-bhatia-ai-human-cx-harmony-middle-east/)).

## Part 5: Step-by-Step Guide to Building & Iterating in Google Antigravity

[Google Antigravity](https://antigravity.google/docs/overview/) is an agent-first development platform that coordinates autonomous Gemini agents across your **Code Editor**, **Terminal Sandbox**, and a **Live Sandboxed Chrome Browser**. Follow these six steps to execute the revamp smoothly:

1. **Step 1 — Open Your Project & Enable Web Plugins**:
   * Open the **Google Antigravity App** (or run `agy` in the Antigravity CLI inside your repository) ([Getting Started Docs](https://antigravity.google/docs/getting-started/)).
   * Go to `Settings > Customizations > Build with Google Plugins` and enable **Modern Web Guidance** (accessibility, Core Web Vitals, security) and **Chrome DevTools** (live Puppeteer DOM inspection and layout verification) ([Build with Google Docs](https://antigravity.google/docs/build-with-google/)).
2. **Step 2 — Lock In Minimalist Design Rules via `AGENTS.md`**:
   * Create a file named `AGENTS.md` in your workspace root ([Rules Docs](https://antigravity.google/docs/rules/)). Paste the **Tech Stack & Design System Rules** and **Dual-Engine CTA Architecture** sections from Part 4 into `AGENTS.md` with `trigger: always_on` in the YAML frontmatter. This guarantees every agent and subagent strictly enforces `CXdynamIQ` casing, `https://cxq360.ai` trial routing, `1px` hairline borders, and zero emoji badges on every turn.
3. **Step 3 — Generate & Approve the Implementation Plan (`/plan`)**:
   * Switch Antigravity into **Plan Mode** (`Shift + Tab` or type `/plan`) and paste the **Master Command** from Part 4 ([Getting Started Docs](https://antigravity.google/docs/getting-started/)).
   * Antigravity will produce an **Implementation Plan Artifact** in the review panel (`Ctrl + R`). You can leave Google-Doc-style inline comments on any section of the plan before clicking **Approve** ([Artifacts Docs](https://antigravity.google/docs/artifacts/)).
4. **Step 4 — Dispatch Parallel Subagents via Agent Manager (`/teamwork`)**:
   * Once approved, use `/teamwork` (or the **Agent Manager** view) so Antigravity spawns parallel subagents in isolated Git worktrees ([Overview Docs](https://antigravity.google/docs/overview/)):
     * **Subagent 1:** Builds the global layout, sticky glass navbar, Fold 1 Hero Simulator, and Fold 2 Marquee.
     * **Subagent 2:** Builds Fold 3 (3 Operating Modes), Fold 4 (WhatsApp-to-Voice Context Engine), and Fold 5 (Co-Pilot Desktop).
     * **Subagent 3:** Builds Fold 6 (100% AI QA Scorecards), Fold 7 (BI Bento Grid), Fold 8 (Industry Tabs), Fold 9 (CXQ360.ai Pricing), and Fold 10 (FAQ & Callback Footer).
5. **Step 5 — Visually Verify & Comment in Live Chrome (`/browser`)**:
   * Prompt Antigravity: *"Start the local dev server and verify all 10 folds in `/browser` across desktop (1440px) and mobile (390px) viewports."*
   * Antigravity's built-in **Sandboxed Chrome Browser** will click through your interactive tabs (`Voice AI`, `WhatsApp -> Voice Handoff`, `100% Auto-QA`), verify that all `Launch Live Sandbox` buttons link properly to `https://cxq360.ai`, and capture **Visual Screenshot Artifacts** and **Browser Recordings** ([Frontend Use Case](https://antigravity.google/use-cases/frontend/)).
   * Click directly on any screenshot artifact to drop a visual comment (e.g., *"Make the Bento grid border slightly brighter on hover"*) and the agent will patch the Tailwind code and re-verify automatically.
6. **Step 6 — Export or Deploy**:
   * If hosting on **Hostinger**, tell Antigravity: *"Configure `output: 'export'` in `next.config.mjs` (or build a static Vite production bundle in `dist/`), verify zero broken links or layout shifts, and zip the production build for Hostinger upload"*—or deploy directly to **Firebase App Hosting** / **Google Cloud Run** using the built-in Google Cloud plugin ([Build with Google Docs](https://antigravity.google/docs/build-with-google/)).
