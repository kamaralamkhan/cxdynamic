This proposal is for redesigning the CXdynamIQ website so it clearly presents the company as an AI-powered customer experience platform — moving away from how a traditional call-center company looks and reads. The existing CXQ360.ai platform stays exactly as it is; this project only touches the main marketing website.
Nothing gets built until you've seen and approved the page structure and homepage layout — that's step one, and it's covered at no risk to you before the rest of the build begins.

| 01 | What We'll Build |
| --- | --- |

A modern, mobile-friendly website that explains your platform clearly within seconds of landing on it.
Core pages: Home, Platform, AI-Powered CX, Omnichannel Engagement, Contact Center, Agent Experience, AI Quality & Conversation Intelligence, Analytics, Agentic AI, Human + AI Collaboration, Integrations.
Individual pages for each industry you serve — banking, insurance, telecom, healthcare, retail and others, as finalized in the sitemap.
A clear path to conversion throughout — Book a Demo / Start Free Trial buttons that lead to CXQ360.ai.
Three rounds of revisions included, with flexibility if minor tweaks come up after.

| 02 | Design Direction |
| --- | --- |

Clean, modern look in line with enterprise AI platforms like NICE, Genesys and Exotel — used only as inspiration, never copied.
Visuals that show your product in action: dashboard screenshots, customer-journey diagrams, and simple flow illustrations rather than generic stock images.
Strong, clear calls to action on every page.




| 03 | Easy Content Editing |
| --- | --- |

Your team can update page text, images and industry pages yourselves — no developer needed for routine changes.
A simple, secure admin panel where changes go live immediately after saving — no delays, no technical steps.
Login protected, with two-factor authentication for extra security.
Page titles and descriptions (for Google search) are editable too.

| 04 | Connecting to CXQ360.ai |
| --- | --- |

When someone clicks Book a Demo or Start Free Trial, they're taken straight into CXQ360.ai with an account already set up for them.
Needs the API/login details for CXQ360.ai from your side to set this up.

| 05 | Capturing Leads |
| --- | --- |

Contact and demo-request forms placed at the key points where a visitor is ready to act.
Every submission is sent to business email instantly.
Forms are protected against spam and bad input.

| 06 | Security & Speed |
| --- | --- |

Built following standard, well-tested security practices — protection against common attacks, safe handling of passwords and data.
Optimized so pages load quickly on both desktop and mobile.
Technically set up for search engines to find and understand your pages properly (SEO basics — clean URLs, sitemap, proper page structure).
